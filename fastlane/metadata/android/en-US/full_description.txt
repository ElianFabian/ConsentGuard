CookieRejecter detects supported cookie and tracking consent prompts and shows a floating Reject optional choices button. Tap it to start a review. You can pause, resume, or stop longer reviews. When a form needs to be submitted, CookieRejecter asks you to confirm.

On supported English Google consent forms, CookieRejecter checks the purpose and vendor switches and verifies changes visually. The in-app activity log shows the result and named switches it turned off.

Search Apps to skip to exclude individual apps. Built-in protection rules skip recognized banking, payment, email, and chat apps. CookieRejecter has no internet permission, and review processing takes place on your device.
